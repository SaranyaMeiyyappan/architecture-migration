## “Tell me about a time you had to push back on stakeholders to protect system stability or customer impact.”

> [!Important]
>
> PayPal behavioral questions want STAR without sounding rehearsed:
>
> - Situation – what was at stake
> - Tension – disagreement or pushback
> - Action – what YOU did
> - Result – measurable outcome

### Behavioral Answer – Stakeholder Pushback

“During our on-prem to hybrid cloud migration, one challenge was that many teams were comfortable with the existing setup and hesitant to adopt DevOps and cloud-based workflows. There was also pressure from management to temporarily deprioritize the initiative due to changing business priorities.

I pushed back by clearly framing the risk — that continuing with the existing setup would worsen **scalability issues and increase downtime during peak traffic,** which could directly impact customer experience.

Instead of positioning it as a tooling change, I focused the discussion on outcomes: faster recovery during incidents, predictable deployments, and the ability to scale safely during high-traffic events. I also worked closely with the DevOps team to simplify the GitLab pipelines and document them, so ownership could be smoothly transitioned after the project.

As a result, leadership aligned on keeping the migration as a priority, teams gradually adopted the new workflows, and the platform successfully handled peak traffic without downtime after the migration.”
